## 2. Where to Find Leads

There are two types of leads in the admin panel:

### Contact Form Leads
- Go to: **Admin → Contacts**
- URL: https://dsetconsulting.com/admin/contacts
- These are people who filled the **Contact Us** form on the website
- You can see their name, email, phone, company, and message

### Chat Leads
- Go to: **Admin → Chat Leads**
- URL: https://dsetconsulting.com/admin/leads
- These are visitors who showed interest through the **chatbot**
- Categorised as: Hot / Warm / Interested

---

## 3. How Email Notifications Work

When someone submits the contact form on the website:

1. Their details are saved in the database (visible in Admin → Contacts)
2. An automatic email notification is sent to **contact@desetconsulting.com**
3. Subject line will be: `[DSeT] New Contact — <Name> · <Company>`





## 4. How to Write a Blog Post

1. Go to: https://dsetconsulting.com/admin/blog
2. Click **"New Post"** or **"Write"**
3. Fill in:
   - **Title** — headline of the blog
   - **Slug** — URL-friendly name (e.g. `ai-in-healthcare`) — auto-fills usually
   - **Category** — topic area
   - **Content** — write the full blog post
   - **Cover Image** — upload a relevant image
4. Click **Publish** when ready
5. Post will be live at: `https://dsetconsulting.com/blog/<slug>`
